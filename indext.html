About Me

Building with intent, pixel by pixel.

NEXORA is my personal corner of the web — a space where I explore the space between design and engineering. As a student developer, I’m constantly learning how great interfaces are built: not just visually, but structurally.

I’m drawn to the craft side of frontend work — typography, layout rhythm, motion, and interaction. Every project I take on is an exercise in turning a rough idea into something structured, responsive, and considered.
